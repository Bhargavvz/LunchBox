# Store listing text

## Parent app: LunchBox

**Short description (80):** Home-cooked lunch, collected from your door and handed over at school on time.

**Full description:**

You pack it at home. A verified LunchBox rider collects it with your pickup code and hands it over at your child's
school gate before lunch, with a photo to show it arrived.

- Add your children and their school once; LunchBox shows the school's timings, working days and holidays.
- Choose a plan and the days you want pickups. Skip a day from the calendar until 9 pm the evening before.
- Follow the lunchbox on a map while it is on the way, and get a notification at every step.
- Pay securely with UPI, cards or net banking through Razorpay. Download receipts any time.
- Your phone number is never shown to riders. Download or delete your data from the app.

Available in English and Hindi. Serving Hyderabad.

## Rider app: LunchBox Partner

**Short description (80):** Your daily lunchbox route: pickups, navigation, school handovers and earnings.

**Full description:**

For LunchBox delivery partners approved by our operations team.

- See today's pickups in route order with instructions for each home.
- Navigate the whole route in Google Maps.
- Confirm each pickup with the parent's code or QR, and each school handover with a photo.
- Hand back an order before you set off if you cannot take it.
- Track your daily and monthly earnings and payouts.

Location is shared only while your route is active.
